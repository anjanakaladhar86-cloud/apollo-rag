4. INSURANCE PRE-AUTHORIZATION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 WHAT IS PRE-AUTHORIZATION?

Pre-authorization (also called prior approval or PA) is the process by which
Apollo Hospitals seeks formal approval from your insurer or TPA before providing
treatment, confirming that:
  - Your policy is active and valid for the date of treatment
  - The proposed treatment/procedure is covered under your policy
  - An estimated amount is approved for cashless settlement

Pre-authorization does NOT guarantee full coverage — final claim settlement
depends on actual treatment provided, policy sub-limits, and insurer review.

4.2 PRE-AUTHORIZATION FOR PLANNED ADMISSIONS

  Step 1 — Pre-admission Preparation (Patient's Responsibility)
    Before arriving for admission, ensure you have:
      - Original insurance card / e-card / policy certificate
      - Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, passport, or driving licence)
      - Policy number and insurer/TPA contact details
      - Pre-operative investigation reports (if already done at another centre)
      - Referral letter from treating consultant (if required by your insurer)
      - CKYC number or KYC documents (for certain insurers)

  Step 2 — TPA Desk Submission (Day of Admission)
    - Submit all documents at the Medical Insurance & TPA Desk.
    - TPA Desk will verify your policy status via the insurer's online portal
      or helpline.
    - A Pre-Authorization Request Form will be raised, including:
        * Patient demographics and policy details
        * Admitting diagnosis (ICD-10 code)
        * Proposed procedure (CPT/ICD-10-PCS code)
        * Estimated length of stay and cost breakup
        * Treating consultant's name and MCI registration number
    - You will receive an acknowledgement reference number for tracking.

  Step 3 — Authorization Receipt
    - Authorization is typically received within:
        * 4-6 hours for planned elective procedures
        * 2-4 hours for urgent admissions
        * 30-60 minutes for emergency life-threatening cases
          (provisional authorization with 24-hour retrospective submission)
    - TPA Desk notifies the patient/attendant and the ward upon receipt.
    - Authorization letter is uploaded to HealthConnect and filed in the
      patient's billing record.
    - If authorization is delayed beyond these timelines, the TPA Desk will
      follow up with the insurer every hour and keep the patient informed.

  Step 4 — Authorization Limits and Enhancements
    - Initial authorization covers the estimated treatment cost for the first
      approved period (typically 5-7 days or until surgery).
    - If admission extends beyond the authorized period or cost, TPA Desk
      raises an enhancement request with updated clinical summary.
    - Enhancement requests are submitted at least 24 hours before expiry.
    - Patients are notified of any coverage gaps or non-covered items.

4.3 PRE-AUTHORIZATION FOR EMERGENCY ADMISSIONS

  - In a genuine medical emergency, treatment will begin immediately
    regardless of insurance status — delay for authorization is not permitted.
  - The patient or attendant must inform the Emergency Reception about
    insurance coverage within 2 hours of admission.
  - TPA Desk will raise a retrospective/emergency authorization within 24
    hours of admission.
  - If the insurer mandates pre-authorization and it is not obtained, the claim
    may be processed as reimbursement rather than cashless — the patient will
    be advised accordingly.

4.4 DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR PRE-AUTHORIZATION

  Mandatory:
    [ ] Insurance card (original or digital copy)
    [ ] Valid photo ID of the patient (Aadhaar preferred)
    [ ] Policy document or certificate of insurance
    [ ] Referral letter from treating physician (if applicable)

  Clinical Documents (provided by Apollo):
    [ ] Admitting consultant's clinical summary
    [ ] Provisional diagnosis and treatment plan
    [ ] Relevant investigation reports (blood work, imaging, ECG)
    [ ] OT notes / procedure plan (for surgical cases)
    [ ] Implant details (brand, model, cost) for orthopedic/cardiac cases

  Additional for Government Schemes (CMCHIS / PMJAY):
    [ ] Scheme beneficiary card or Aadhaar-linked e-card
    [ ] Family composition certificate (if applicable)
    [ ] BPL certificate or income certificate (scheme-dependent)

5. CASHLESS HOSPITALIZATION — PROCESS OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 HOW CASHLESS WORKS

In a cashless arrangement, Apollo Hospitals directly bills the insurer/TPA for
covered expenses. The patient does not pay the insured amount at discharge —
only non-covered or excess amounts are collected from the patient.

5.2 PATIENT RESPONSIBILITIES UNDER CASHLESS

  At Admission:
    - Submit complete documents at TPA Desk (see Section 4.4)
    - Sign the Hospital Admission and Authorization Form
    - Pay a refundable security deposit as applicable (see Section 7.2)
    - Pay any co-payment required by your policy upfront

  During Stay:
    - Inform TPA Desk promptly of any change in diagnosis or treatment plan
    - Do not use room amenities or services beyond your entitled category
      without checking coverage implications

  At Discharge:
    - Review the final bill carefully before signing
    - Pay non-covered items, deductibles, and co-payment (if any)
    - Collect your discharge summary, all reports, and pharmacy receipts

5.3 COMMON NON-COVERED ITEMS (PATIENT PAYS)

  Regardless of insurance type, the following are typically NOT covered and
  must be paid by the patient:

    - Registration / admission charges
    - Attendant charges and extra bed charges
    - Food and beverages beyond standard hospital meals
    - Telephone and TV charges
    - Personal hygiene items (toiletries, diapers)
    - Non-formulary / cosmetic medications
    - Spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids (unless policy-specific rider)
    - Charges for complications arising from non-covered conditions
    - OPD consultations and day-care procedures (unless covered)
    - Charges exceeding room rent sub-limit (proportionate deduction may apply)

  IMPORTANT: Room Rent Sub-Limits
    Many policies have room rent caps (e.g., Rs. 3,000/day for a standard
    private room). If you choose a room above your entitled category, ALL
    associated charges (consultant fees, nursing, OT, ICU) may be
    proportionately reduced by the insurer. Consult the TPA Desk before
    selecting your room.

6. REIMBURSEMENT CLAIMS — SUPPORT AND DOCUMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 WHEN REIMBURSEMENT APPLIES

  - Your insurer is not empanelled with Apollo Hospitals Chennai
  - Pre-authorization was denied or not obtained (emergency)
  - Policy terms require post-treatment reimbursement
  - You choose to pay and claim independently

6.2 CLAIM SUPPORT PACKAGE PROVIDED BY APOLLO

  Apollo Hospitals Chennai provides the following documents for reimbursement
  claims (obtainable from Patient Accounts Desk, Ground Floor, up to 30 days
  post-discharge):

    [ ] Original Discharge Summary (signed, stamped, and with hospital letterhead)
    [ ] Itemized final bill (original with hospital stamp and authorized signature)
    [ ] All investigation reports (laboratory and radiology — originals or
        certified true copies)
    [ ] Operation Theatre notes and anaesthesia record (for surgical cases)
    [ ] Pharmacy bills (itemized, with Rx reference)
    [ ] X-ray / MRI / CT films or CDs (with patient name and date)
    [ ] Pre-authorization reference number (if obtained)
    [ ] Hospital registration certificate copy (for first-time claims)
    [ ] Implant stickers / device invoices (for joint replacements, stents, etc.)
    [ ] Treating consultant's clinical certificate (on request, within 7 working days)

  Note: Duplicate copies are chargeable at Rs. 200 per document. Requests for
  records post-30 days require 7 working days' processing time.

6.3 CLAIM SUBMISSION TIPS

  - Submit your claim to the insurer within 30 days of discharge (check your
    policy for exact deadline — some policies allow 60 or 90 days).
  - Keep photocopies of all documents submitted to the insurer.
  - Obtain an acknowledgement with claim reference number from the insurer.
  - Follow up with insurer after 15 working days if no update is received.
  - Apollo's TPA Desk can write a supportive clinical letter to the insurer
    if your claim is queried — contact insurance.chennai@apollohospitals.com.

7. BILLING PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 BILL GENERATION PROCESS

  Apollo Hospitals Chennai uses a fully integrated billing system via the
  HealthConnect EMR and Hospital Management System (HMS). All charges are
  captured in real time.

  Stages of Billing:
    Stage 1 — Interim Bill:
      - Available on request at any point during hospitalization.
      - Useful for insurance enhancement requests and financial planning.
      - Request at the ward Billing Help Point or Patient Accounts (ext. 3205).

    Stage 2 — Pre-Discharge Bill:
      - Prepared 4-6 hours before anticipated discharge.
      - Sent to TPA Desk for insurance settlement (cashless) or to the patient
        for review (self-pay/reimbursement).

    Stage 3 — Final Bill:
      - Generated after treating consultant confirms discharge in HealthConnect.
      - Includes all charges: consultation, nursing, procedures, OT, ICU, pharmacy,
        investigations, consumables, accommodation, and miscellaneous.
      - Any adjustments require written approval from Billing Manager (ext. 3201).

7.2 SECURITY DEPOSITS

  A refundable security deposit is collected at admission based on the estimated
  length of stay and nature of treatment:

    Category                          | Deposit Amount
    ----------------------------------|---------------------------
    General Ward Admission            | Rs. 10,000 – Rs. 25,000
    Private / Deluxe Room Admission   | Rs. 25,000 – Rs. 75,000
    ICU Admission                     | Rs. 75,000 – Rs. 1,50,000
    Cardiac / Neurosurgery            | Rs. 1,00,000 – Rs. 2,50,000
    Major Oncology Procedures         | Rs. 50,000 – Rs. 1,50,000

    - Deposit is adjusted against the final bill.
    - Excess deposit refunded at discharge (cash refund > Rs. 5,000 requires
      2 working days via NEFT; immediate refund for card/UPI payments).
    - For cashless patients: deposit may be reduced or waived upon presentation
      of confirmed pre-authorization letter.

7.3 ITEMIZED BILLING — YOUR RIGHT

  Every patient has the right to receive an itemized bill. Apollo Hospitals
  provides itemized bills as the default. The bill will include:

    - Room and accommodation charges (per day)
    - Consultant visit charges (each visit itemized)
    - Nursing care charges
    - Procedure / OT / ICU charges
    - Anaesthesia charges
    - Pharmacy (each medicine listed with quantity and unit cost)
    - Laboratory investigations (each test listed)
    - Radiology investigations (each study listed)
    - Physiotherapy / dietetics (if applicable)
    - Implants and medical devices (with make, model, and MRP)
    - Miscellaneous (oxygen, blood transfusion, specialist charges)

  If you believe any item is incorrect:
    - Raise the query at the Billing Desk before signing the bill.
    - Billing Desk will verify with the clinical team within 2 hours.
    - A formal billing dispute must be submitted in writing to the Billing
      Manager (ext. 3201) if not resolved at the counter.
    - Apollo guarantees resolution of billing disputes within 24 hours.

7.4 PACKAGE RATES

  Apollo Hospitals Chennai offers all-inclusive package rates for many common
  procedures. Package rates cover:
    - Surgery charges
    - OT and anaesthesia
    - Standard implant cost (if applicable)
    - Post-operative ward stay (up to stated duration)
    - Routine post-operative medications

  Packages do NOT cover:
    - Pre-operative investigations (unless specified)
    - ICU stay beyond the included duration
    - Blood and blood products
    - Complications requiring extended stay or re-surgery
    - Specialist consultations beyond the primary surgeon

  Package rates are available at the Patient Accounts Desk and on the Apollo
  Hospitals Chennai website under "Packages & Pricing."

9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. Will Apollo inform my insurer if my treatment plan changes during
    hospitalization?

  Yes. The TPA Desk actively monitors ongoing admissions. If the treating
  consultant changes the diagnosis, adds a procedure, or the patient is
  transferred to ICU, TPA Desk submits an enhancement request to the insurer.
  However, it is also the patient's responsibility to keep the TPA Desk informed
  of significant changes. TPA Desk can be reached at ext. 3101 or by the
  ward nurse directly.

Q2. My insurer has given authorization for Rs. 1,50,000 but my final bill is
    Rs. 2,10,000. What happens?

  You will be responsible for the Rs. 60,000 difference. TPA Desk will submit
  the final bill to the insurer and collect the balance from you at discharge.
  If the excess is due to a change in treatment, TPA Desk will attempt to obtain
  an enhanced authorization, but this is not guaranteed. In some cases, post-
  discharge enhancement requests may be submitted and the balance refunded if
  approved.

Q3. My policy has a co-payment clause. When do I pay my share?

  Co-payment amounts are collected at discharge along with any non-covered items.
  The TPA Desk will calculate your co-payment based on the final authorized
  amount. Co-payment percentages vary (typically 10%–20%); refer to your policy
  document.

Q4. Can I be shifted to a higher room category without affecting my coverage?

  Not without consequences. If your policy has a room rent sub-limit, choosing
  a room above the limit causes proportionate deductions across all associated
  charges. Always check with the TPA Desk BEFORE upgrading your room.

Q5. My insurer is not empanelled with Apollo. Can I still get admitted?

  Yes. You will be admitted on a self-pay basis. Apollo will provide all
  documents needed for reimbursement from your insurer after discharge.
  Refer to Section 6 for the full list of documents provided.

Q6. How do I get my Discharge Summary and reports if I need them later?

  Discharge summaries and investigation reports can be collected from:
    - Patient Accounts Desk (Ground Floor) within 30 days of discharge at
      no charge (1 original copy).
    - Apollo's patient portal (MyApollo app or apollohospitals.com) — available
      digitally within 48 hours of finalization.
  Beyond 30 days, a processing fee of Rs. 200 per document applies and
  documents will be ready within 7 working days.

Q7. I was denied cashless authorization. Can I appeal?

  Yes. The TPA Desk will provide you with the denial letter from the insurer
  citing the reason. You may:
    (a) Request the treating consultant to provide additional clinical justification.
        The TPA Desk will re-submit to the insurer with supporting documentation.
    (b) Contact your insurer's grievance cell directly (contact on denial letter).
    (c) Escalate to the Insurance Regulatory Authority of India (IRDAI)
        grievance portal: igms.irda.gov.in if the insurer does not resolve
        within 15 working days.
  Apollo's TPA Desk will provide all supporting documentation for your appeal.

Q8. Can Apollo hold me at the hospital if my insurance doesn't cover the full amount?

  No. Apollo Hospitals Chennai does not withhold patients pending insurance
  clearance. You may be discharged and asked to settle the uncovered amount.
  If you face financial hardship, the Medical Social Worker (ext. 4412) can
  assist with exploring options including Apollo's patient assistance programs.

Q9. I was admitted under AB-PMJAY (Ayushman Bharat). What is covered?

  Under AB-PMJAY, eligible patients receive cashless treatment for covered
  procedures up to Rs. 5 lakhs per family per year. Covered procedures are
  defined by the AB-PMJAY Health Benefit Package (HBP) and are listed at the
  PMJAY Desk (adjacent to TPA Desk). Treatment beyond the HBP package requires
  self-payment. Beneficiary verification is done via the PMJAY portal using
  Aadhaar-based authentication.

Q10. What is CMCHIS and how does it work at Apollo?

  The Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS) is the
  Tamil Nadu government scheme providing cashless treatment up to Rs. 5 lakhs
  (or Rs. 10 lakhs for selected conditions) for eligible Tamil Nadu residents.
  Apollo Hospitals Chennai is a CMCHIS-empanelled hospital.

  To use CMCHIS at Apollo:
    - Present your CMCHIS smart card and Aadhaar at the Government Schemes Desk
      (Ground Floor, adjacent to Main Emergency).
    - Biometric authentication will be completed.
    - Only procedures listed in the CMCHIS treatment package are covered cashless.
    - Non-package procedures require self-payment.
  CMCHIS Desk timings: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
  CMCHIS Helpline: 044-2829-3333 Ext. 3110.

Q11. My bill seems higher than the published package rate. Can I raise a dispute?

  Yes. Package applicability depends on the clinical scenario at the time of
  treatment. Complications, ICU care, or extended stays may move the case outside
  the package. The Billing Manager (ext. 3201) will provide a written explanation
  and reconciliation within 24 hours of a formal dispute being raised.

Q12. Is GST applicable on my hospital bill?

  Healthcare services provided in hospitals are exempt from GST as per the
  Central Tax (Rate) Notification No. 12/2017. However, some ancillary services
  (e.g., food, cafeteria, cosmetic procedures) may attract GST. Your itemized
  bill will reflect any applicable GST separately for transparency.

11. ANTI-FRAUD ADVISORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Apollo Hospitals Chennai strongly advises patients against:

  - Submitting false or inflated claims to insurers.
  - Claiming treatment for non-admitted or non-treated family members.
  - Requesting backdated or altered documents.

Such acts constitute insurance fraud and are punishable under the Indian Penal
Code (Sections 415, 417, 420) and the Insurance Act, 1938. Apollo is legally
obligated to report suspected fraud to the insurer and law enforcement authorities.

All staff are required to refuse any request to falsify records and report such
requests immediately to the Compliance Officer (ext. 4500).
