1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy establishes a standardized framework for the safe and timely discharge
of patients from Apollo Hospitals Chennai across all wards and care units. The
procedures outlined herein ensure continuity of care, regulatory compliance, patient
safety, and efficient bed management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy applies to:
  - All inpatient units including General Wards, Semi-Private, Private, and Deluxe rooms
  - Intensive Care Units (ICU, MICU, SICU, CICU, NICU, PICU)
  - High Dependency Units (HDU)
  - Day Surgery and Day Care units
  - All clinical, nursing, and administrative staff involved in the discharge process

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Treating Consultant   : The primary physician responsible for clinical decisions
                          regarding the patient's admission and discharge.

  Discharge Summary     : A formal medical document summarizing the patient's
                          diagnosis, treatment, investigations, and follow-up plan.

  Pre-Authorization     : Approval obtained from the patient's insurance provider
                          before or during hospitalization for coverage of treatment costs.

  Final Bill            : The consolidated invoice generated by the billing department
                          upon confirmation of discharge by the treating consultant.

  Discharge Coordinator : Designated staff member (typically a senior nurse or
                          case manager) responsible for facilitating the discharge
                          process end-to-end.

4. ICU PATIENT DISCHARGE PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 CRITERIA FOR ICU DISCHARGE

A patient may be considered for discharge from the ICU when the following
clinical criteria are met and documented by the treating intensivist:

  (a) Hemodynamic Stability
      - Stable blood pressure without vasopressor support (or on minimal,
        tapering doses as approved by the intensivist)
      - Heart rate within acceptable range for the patient's condition
      - No new or unexplained cardiac arrhythmias requiring continuous monitoring

  (b) Respiratory Stability
      - Patient is successfully weaned from mechanical ventilation (if applicable)
      - Adequate oxygenation on supplemental oxygen of <=4L/min via nasal cannula,
        or on room air
      - No signs of acute respiratory distress

  (c) Neurological Status
      - Patient is alert, oriented, or at their baseline neurological status
      - No acute seizure activity in the preceding 24 hours
      - GCS score >= 12 (unless chronic baseline is lower, as documented)

  (d) Renal and Metabolic Status
      - Urine output >= 0.5 mL/kg/hr without continuous renal support therapy
      - Electrolytes within acceptable limits or on stable oral/IV correction
      - Blood glucose manageable on subcutaneous insulin (not continuous IV infusion)

  (e) Infection Control
      - Afebrile for >= 24 hours, OR fever documented with a known source
        under appropriate antimicrobial therapy
      - No new positive blood cultures requiring escalation of treatment

4.2 ICU DISCHARGE PROCESS - STEP BY STEP

  Step 1 - Intensivist Decision (D-Day)
    The intensivist documents the discharge decision in the Electronic Medical
    Record (EMR) system (Apollo HealthConnect) by 09:00 AM where possible.
    The note must include:
      - Clinical justification for ICU step-down
      - Destination ward/unit
      - Pending investigations to be monitored post-transfer
      - Any residual monitoring requirements

  Step 2 - Receiving Ward Notification
    The ICU Charge Nurse contacts the receiving ward (HDU, General Ward, or
    private room) to confirm bed availability and estimated time of transfer.
    Bed allocation must be confirmed within 2 hours of the step-down order.

  Step 3 - Nursing Handover
    A formal nursing handover (SBAR format - Situation, Background, Assessment,
    Recommendation) is conducted between the ICU nurse and the receiving ward
    nurse. This must occur in person at the bedside or via structured handover
    documentation in HealthConnect.

  Step 4 - Transfer of Patient
    - All IV lines, drains, and monitoring leads are assessed and secured prior
      to transfer.
    - The patient is transferred with a portable monitor if SpO2 monitoring is
      required in transit.
    - A nurse or nursing aide must accompany the patient during transfer.
    - Transfer must be logged in HealthConnect with the time and receiving nurse's ID.

  Step 5 - Final Discharge from Hospital (Post-ICU Step-Down)
    Once the patient has been stabilized in the step-down unit, the general
    discharge process (Section 6) is followed for final discharge from the hospital.

4.3 ICU DISCHARGE - DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

  The following must be completed before an ICU patient is transferred:
    [ ] ICU Discharge Summary (interim) signed by the intensivist in HealthConnect
    [ ] Active problem list updated
    [ ] Medication reconciliation completed
    [ ] Nursing handover form (AH-CHN-FORM-ICU-019) completed and signed
    [ ] Family/next-of-kin informed of transfer
    [ ] Pending labs/radiology flagged in HealthConnect for receiving team

5. INSURANCE PRE-AUTHORIZATION & DISCHARGE CLEARANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 OVERVIEW

Apollo Hospitals Chennai is empanelled with over 40 insurance providers and
Third-Party Administrators (TPAs). The Medical Insurance & TPA Desk (located
on the Ground Floor, near Main Reception) manages all insurance-related
discharge activities.

5.2 PRE-AUTHORIZATION PROCESS

  Phase 1 - Initial Authorization (At Admission)
    - The patient or attendant must submit the insurance card, photo ID, and
      policy documents at the TPA Desk within 4 hours of admission.
    - The TPA Desk will raise an initial pre-authorization request with the
      insurer/TPA, including:
        * Provisional diagnosis
        * Proposed treatment plan and estimated cost
        * Admitting consultant's name and registration number
    - Authorization is typically received within 6-12 hours for planned
      admissions and within 2-4 hours for emergency admissions.

  Phase 2 - Enhancement Request (During Stay)
    - If the patient's treatment extends beyond the initially authorized amount
      or duration, the TPA Desk will submit an enhancement request.
    - The treating consultant must provide an updated clinical summary and
      justification for continued hospitalization.
    - Enhancement requests must be submitted at least 24 hours before the
      existing authorization expires.
    - The nursing station will alert the TPA Desk if ICU stay exceeds 5 days,
      to trigger a mandatory review.

  Phase 3 - Final Authorization & Discharge Clearance
    - On the day prior to anticipated discharge, the treating consultant
      informs the TPA Desk via HealthConnect (Discharge Intimation Form).
    - The TPA Desk submits the Final Claim Intimation to the insurer, including:
        * Final diagnosis (ICD-10 coded)
        * Complete procedure list (CPT/ICD-10-PCS codes)
        * Summary of investigations, implants, and consumables
        * Discharge summary (draft)
    - Insurance clearance must be obtained before the final bill is generated.
    - In cases of delay from the insurer (>4 hours on the day of discharge),
      the patient may be discharged on a provisional basis with a co-payment
      deposit, subject to Medical Administrator approval.

5.3 INSURANCE COMPANY / TPA CONTACTS

  The TPA Desk maintains an updated contact directory for all empanelled
  insurers. Key contacts include (representative list — refer to TPA Desk
  for full and current list):

    - Star Health and Allied Insurance
    - United India Insurance
    - New India Assurance
    - ICICI Lombard General Insurance
    - Medi Assist TPA
    - Health India TPA
    - Vipul MedCorp TPA
    - MD India Health Insurance TPA

5.4 CASHLESS VS. REIMBURSEMENT CLAIMS

  Cashless Discharge:
    - Patient does not pay the insured amount at discharge.
    - Applicable only when pre-authorization has been approved for cashless.
    - Patient is responsible only for non-covered items (e.g., personal items,
      non-formulary medications, registration charges, food upgrades).

  Reimbursement Claims:
    - Patient pays the full bill at discharge.
    - TPA Desk provides a claim support package:
        * Discharge summary (signed and stamped)
        * Itemized bill
        * All original investigation reports
        * Doctor's prescriptions
        * Pharmacy receipts
        * Pre-authorization reference number (if obtained)
    - Reimbursement claims must be submitted to the insurer within 30 days
      of discharge (as per most policy terms; patient to verify with insurer).

5.5 DENIED OR PARTIAL AUTHORIZATION

  If authorization is denied or partially approved:
    - The TPA Desk Coordinator will notify the patient's attendant immediately.
    - The Medical Social Worker (ext. 4412) will assist families who face
      financial hardship in identifying alternate payment options.
    - The patient may appeal the denial through the insurer's grievance process;
      Apollo's TPA Desk will provide supporting documentation upon request.
    - Denial of insurance does not delay clinical discharge — billing will
      proceed on a self-pay basis if required.

6. GENERAL DISCHARGE CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following checklist must be completed for ALL patient discharges from
Apollo Hospitals Chennai. Responsibility is shared between the treating team,
nursing staff, and administrative staff as indicated.

6.1 CLINICAL TEAM RESPONSIBILITIES

  [ ] Discharge decision documented in HealthConnect by treating consultant
  [ ] Discharge Summary completed, signed, and finalized in HealthConnect
        - Must include: Admission diagnosis, final diagnosis (ICD-10),
          summary of treatment, investigations, procedures, complications
          (if any), condition at discharge, and follow-up instructions
  [ ] Medication reconciliation completed
        - Discharge prescription written legibly (or generated via HealthConnect)
        - Generic names used where possible; brand names specified if clinically necessary
        - Duration and dosage clearly mentioned for all medications
  [ ] Follow-up appointments scheduled
        - OPD appointment booked in HealthConnect (or patient advised to call 044-2829-0200)
        - Referral letters issued if follow-up with another specialist is required
  [ ] Patient / caregiver counselling completed (documented in nursing notes)
        - Wound care instructions (if applicable)
        - Diet and activity restrictions
        - Red flag symptoms that require immediate return to ER
        - Physiotherapy or rehabilitation instructions (if applicable)
  [ ] Pending investigation results reviewed or flagged for follow-up
  [ ] Removal of IV cannulas, urinary catheters, and other invasive lines confirmed

6.2 NURSING TEAM RESPONSIBILITIES

  [ ] Vital signs stable and documented within 2 hours of discharge
  [ ] Patient able to ambulate independently or with assistance (as applicable)
  [ ] Patient/caregiver demonstrates understanding of home care instructions
  [ ] Discharge education sheet (AH-CHN-FORM-EDU-007) provided in preferred language
        - Available in: English, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam
  [ ] All belongings returned to patient (valuables released from safe if deposited)
  [ ] Patient identification band removed at discharge
  [ ] Bed and room sanitized and reported to housekeeping for turnover
  [ ] Discharge logged in ward register and HealthConnect

6.3 BILLING & ADMINISTRATIVE RESPONSIBILITIES

  [ ] Insurance clearance obtained (for cashless patients) - TPA Desk
  [ ] Final bill generated and reviewed for accuracy
        - Itemized bill provided to patient/attendant upon request
        - Any billing discrepancies escalated to Billing Manager (ext. 3201)
  [ ] Payment collected or confirmed (cash, card, UPI, or insurance)
  [ ] Official receipt issued
  [ ] Discharge Summary original provided to patient (1 copy retained in file)
  [ ] Investigation reports and films (X-rays, MRI CDs) handed over
  [ ] Medico-legal documents retained in file (if case is ML)
  [ ] Patient feedback form offered (digital via Apollo App or paper form)

6.4 PHARMACY - DISCHARGE MEDICATIONS

  [ ] Discharge prescription sent to Apollo Pharmacy (Ground Floor or Ward Satellite)
  [ ] Medications dispensed and handed to patient/attendant
  [ ] Pharmacist counselling completed for high-risk medications
        (anticoagulants, insulin, immunosuppressants, narrow therapeutic index drugs)
  [ ] Patient advised on medication storage requirements (refrigeration, etc.)
  [ ] Apollo Pharmacy contact for queries: 044-2829-0300 (7 AM - 10 PM daily)

7. DISCHARGE TIMING & BED MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Target discharge time: 12:00 Noon (for planned discharges)
  - Discharge orders must be entered in HealthConnect by 10:00 AM to meet
    the 12:00 Noon target.
  - Late discharges (after 2:00 PM) may attract an additional half-day room charge
    as per the hospital's tariff policy, unless delayed due to hospital processes.
  - The Bed Management Team (ext. 4100) coordinates with all wards to ensure
    timely bed turnover and availability for new admissions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 DISCHARGE AGAINST MEDICAL ADVICE (DAMA)

  If a patient wishes to leave against medical advice:
    - The treating consultant must personally counsel the patient/family on risks.
    - The patient (or legal guardian) must sign the DAMA form (AH-CHN-FORM-DAMA-001).
    - A discharge summary with "DAMA" notation must still be prepared.
    - The duty medical officer must be informed.
    - Insurance coverage may be affected — TPA Desk must be notified immediately.

8.2 DEATH & MEDICAL CERTIFICATE

  - If a patient expires in the hospital, this policy does not apply for discharge.
  - Refer to policy AH-CHN-MLC-003 (Death in Hospital - Procedures) for next steps.
  - The Medical Certificate of Cause of Death (MCCD) will be issued within 24 hours
    by the treating consultant or duty medical officer.

8.3 PATIENTS REQUIRING TRANSFER TO ANOTHER FACILITY

  - Refer to policy AH-CHN-TRF-002 (Inter-facility Transfer Policy).
  - A transfer summary (not a discharge summary) must be prepared.
  - Ambulance coordination: Contact Apollo Ambulance Services, ext. 4911.

8.4 MEDICO-LEGAL CASES (MLC)

  - MLC patients cannot be discharged without clearance from the duty medical officer
    AND confirmation that the concerned police station has been informed (if required).
  - All MLC documentation must be retained in the hospital file permanently.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. PATIENT RIGHTS AT DISCHARGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every patient discharged from Apollo Hospitals Chennai has the right to:

  - Receive a complete discharge summary in a language they understand
  - Request an itemized bill and have billing queries resolved before payment
  - Receive all original investigation reports and imaging CDs/films
  - Be informed of follow-up care requirements
  - Access the Patient Relations Officer for any grievances
    (Patient Relations: ext. 4050 | patientrelations.chennai@apollohospitals.com)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. POLICY COMPLIANCE & AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Compliance with this policy is monitored by the Quality & Patient Safety team.
  - Discharge process audits are conducted monthly via random chart reviews.
  - Non-compliance must be reported to the Unit Head and Quality Department.
  - This policy is reviewed annually or earlier if regulatory or accreditation
    requirements change (NABH / JCI standards applicable).
